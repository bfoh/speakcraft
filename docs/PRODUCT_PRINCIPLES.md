# SpeakCraft product principles

SpeakCraft helps Ghanaian TVET learners communicate in real vocational situations. The source of truth is the Product & Technical Blueprint and explicit current user instructions.

- Prioritise meaning and task completion before grammatical perfection.
- Make listening and speaking easy, with large labelled controls and short instructions.
- Treat Android and iOS as equal targets, including small screens and large text.
- Respect Ghanaian English; accent variation alone is not an error.
- Let a learner retry without shame or exaggerated praise.
- Preserve work when connectivity changes and distinguish saved navigation from learning progress.
- Keep the five-day Alpha focused on Beauty & Cosmetology.
- Present only measured results. A saved recording is not successful communication or assessment completion.
- Human authors define objectives. AI may eventually adapt practice within them.
- Review translations with native speakers before exposing them as reliable support.

Sprint 1 introduces Kora truthfully as the future AI tutor, provides fixed examples, and explicitly marks assessment/live conversation as unavailable. It does not claim the Alpha learning hypothesis has been validated.
